# Inversion: The Power of Avoiding Stupidity

## 文章总结

这篇文章介绍了“反转”（Inversion）这一心理模型，核心观点是：避免愚蠢比追求卓越更容易，且反转思维是解决复杂问题的重要工具。作者引用查理·芒格的“我只想知道我会死在哪里，所以我永远不会去那里”和数学家雅各比的“反转，永远反转”来说明此法。反转思维意味着从问题的反面去思考，例如，与其思考如何创新，不如思考如何阻碍创新，然后避免这些阻碍因素。这种“减法”思维比“加法”思维（向前思考）更能有效避免负面结果，提高理解力，并帮助人们识别和规避隐藏的负面信念。最终，作者鼓励读者将精力更多地放在避免愚蠢上，以此作为一种简单有效的提升方式。

## 关键要点

- 文章介绍了“反转”（Inversion）这一心理模型。
- 核心观点：避免愚蠢比追求卓越更容易。
- 反转思维是解决复杂问题的有效策略，常被比作“逆向思考”。
- 例子：与其思考如何创新，不如思考如何阻碍创新，然后避免后者。
- 反转是一种“减法”思维，有助于避免负面结果和隐藏的负面信念。
- 建议：花更多时间避免愚蠢，而不是追求卓越。

![Inversion Mental Model](https://fs.blog/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/inversion-thinking-white.jpg)

[Charlie Munger](https://fs.blog/charlie-munger/), the business partner of Warren Buffett and Vice Chairman of Berkshire Hathaway, is famous for his quote “All I want to know is where I’m going to die, so I’ll never go there.” That thinking was inspired by the German mathematician Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi, famous for some work on elliptic functions that I’ll never understand. Jacobi often solved difficult problems by following a simple strategy: “ *man muss* immer umkehren” (or loosely translated, “invert, always invert.”)  
[查理·芒格](https://fs.blog/charlie-munger/) ，沃伦·巴菲特的商业伙伴、伯克希尔·哈撒韦副董事长，以他的名言“我只想知道我会死在哪里，所以我永远不会去那里”而闻名。这种想法受到了德国数学家卡尔·古斯塔夫·雅各布·雅各比的启发，他以椭圆函数方面的一些工作闻名，但我永远无法理解。雅各比常用一个简单的策略来解决难题：“ *man muss* immer umkehren”（大致翻译为“反转，永远反转”）。

“\[Jacobi\] knew that it is in the nature of things that many hard problems are best solved when they are addressed backward,” Munger counsels.  
芒格建议道：“\[雅各比\]知道，许多难题的本质上，最好是逆着来解决。”

While Jacobi applied inversion mostly to mathematics, the model is one of the most powerful [mental models](https://fs.blog/mental-models/) in our toolkit.  
虽然雅各比主要将反演应用于数学，但该模型是我们工具箱中最强大的 [心理模型](https://fs.blog/mental-models/) 之一。

It is not enough to think about difficult problems one way. You need to think about them forwards and backward. Inversion often forces you to uncover hidden beliefs about the problem you are trying to solve. “Indeed,” says Munger, “many problems can’t be solved forward.”  
仅仅以一种方式思考困难问题是不够的。你需要正反思考。倒置常常迫使你揭露关于你试图解决的问题的隐藏信念。芒格说：“确实如此，许多问题无法在未来解决。”

Let’s take a look at some examples. Say you want to improve innovation in your organization. Thinking forward, you’d think about all of the things you could do to foster innovation. If you look at the problem by inversion, however, you’d think about all the things you could do that would discourage innovation. Ideally, you’d avoid those things. Sounds simple right? I bet your organization does some of those ‘stupid’ things today.  
让我们来看一些例子。假设你想提升组织的创新。展望未来，你会思考所有可以促进创新的方法。但如果你用反转来看问题，你会想到所有可能阻碍创新的事情。理想情况下，你应该避免这些事情。听起来很简单，对吧？我敢打赌你们组织现在也做过一些“愚蠢”的事情。

Another example, rather than think about what makes a good life, you can think about what prescriptions would ensure misery.  
另一个例子，与其思考什么造就美好生活，你可以考虑哪些处方药会带来痛苦。

## Avoiding stupidity is easier than seeking brilliance.避免愚蠢比追求卓越更容易。

While both thinking forward and thinking backward result in some action, you can think of them as *additive* vs. *subtractive*.  
虽然向前思考和向后思考都会产生一定的动作，但你可以把它们看作 *是加法* 而非 *减* 法。

Despite our best intentions, thinking forward increases the odds that you’ll cause harm ([iatrogenics](https://fs.blog/2013/10/iatrogenics/)). Thinking backward, call it subtractive avoidance or inversion, is less likely to cause harm.  
尽管我们有最好的意图，但向前思考反而增加了你造成伤害的可能性（医 [源性药物](https://fs.blog/2013/10/iatrogenics/) ）。倒头思考，称之为减法规避或倒置，更不容易造成伤害。

Inverting the problem won’t always solve it, but it will help you avoid trouble. You can think of it as the avoiding stupidity filter. It’s not sexy but it’s a very easy way to improve.  
逆转问题并不总能解决问题，但能帮助你避免麻烦。你可以把它看作是避免愚蠢的过滤器。这不性感，但却是一个非常简单的提升方式。

**So what does this mean in practice?  
那么，这在实际作中意味着什么？**

Spending time thinking about the opposite of what you want doesn’t come naturally to most people. And yet many of the smartest people in history, have done this naturally.  
花时间思考与自己想要相反的事情对大多数人来说并不自然。然而，历史上许多最聪明的人，却是自然而然地做到了这一点。

Inversion helps improve understanding of the problem. By forcing you to do [the work necessary to have an opinion](https://fs.blog/2013/04/the-work-required-to-have-an-opinion/) you’re forced to consider different perspectives.  
倒立有助于加深对问题的理解。强迫你付出 [必要的努力去表达观点](https://fs.blog/2013/04/the-work-required-to-have-an-opinion/) ，你不得不 考虑不同的观点。

If you’re to take anything away from inversion let it be this: Spend less time trying to be brilliant and more time trying to avoid obvious stupidity. The kicker? [Avoiding stupidity is easier than seeking brilliance](https://fs.blog/2014/06/avoiding-stupidity/).  
如果你要从反转中学到什么，那就是：少花时间试图表现得聪明，多花点时间避免明显的愚蠢。最关键的是？避免愚蠢 [比追求卓越更容易](https://fs.blog/2014/06/avoiding-stupidity/) 。

倒置是 [法纳姆街心理模型格子](https://fs.blog/mental-models/) 艺术的一部分。*
